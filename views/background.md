# Background and Supporting Resources

I built this application for two reasons:

1) Unlock data about housing to enable informed discussions
2) To encourage myself and others to learn about the very complex issue that is the "housing crisis" so that we can better contribute to addressing the issue in a meaningful way.

Please do not take any of this work or my discussion about challenges I faced as an indictment or criticism of Exeter's government or policies. The Town government, Select Board, various committees and boards, and volunteers all have been amazingly open to discussion, suggestions, questions, and requests.

Exeter's challenges are by no means unique to Exeter and the various forces that created the current housing environment stretch back decades. (See "Educational Resources").

## Why This Application?
This started as an effort to educate myself about a local housing crisis and I suspect it will be part of an ongoing effort to educate myself about housing in general.

## Data access, data quality

I thought I could just walk into the Town's offices, or go to their web site, and find detailed data about the housing stock in town.
